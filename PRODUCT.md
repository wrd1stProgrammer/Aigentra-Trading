# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI Trader League is used by builders and operators who need to inspect a local paper trading league without touching real exchange accounts. They compare AI trader performance, current paper exposure, scanner state, and management reviews across BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT.

## Product Purpose

The product turns strategy checks, LLM reviews, paper orders, paper positions, and equity snapshots into a readable operating console. Success means the user can quickly answer: who is leading, where risk is active, what the AI last decided, and which trader needs deeper inspection.

## Brand Personality

Precise, controlled, analytical. The interface should feel like a serious trading operations tool, not a marketing site and not a toy crypto dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS hero layouts, nested rounded cards, decorative gradients, chart mockups that hide real data, weak contrast, oversized text, and color semantics where profit appears red or loss appears green.

## Design Principles

1. Put live paper-trading state before explanation.
2. Make rank, PnL, exposure, and AI decision visible without page hunting.
3. Treat charts and tables as the product, not decoration.
4. Use restrained color with strict semantic meaning: green for favorable, red for unfavorable, amber for warning, neutral for idle.
5. Keep Korean and English copy compact enough for dense trading screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Korean and English, light and dark themes, keyboard-reachable controls, clear focus states, reduced-motion preferences, and text contrast suitable for dashboard use.
